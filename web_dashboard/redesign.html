About the data

Sources & calculation rules

Each chart counts a different group of movies

The highest-rated highlight uses films with 100,000+ votes on either source, with IMDb rating preferred. The most-voted highlight uses the largest independently preferred vote count among rated titles.

The catalog includes IMDb movie and TV-movie records plus current TMDB enrichment. Adult content is not excluded. TMDB enrichment covers the current United States and India now-playing extract, not the full TMDB catalog.

Ratings prefer IMDb when known and otherwise use TMDB. Vote counts make that choice independently, so a displayed rating and its vote count can come from different sources. Averages give each contributing row equal weight; they are not weighted by votes.

Role selection changes only the people chart and table. Market selection changes only now-playing recommendations. These aggregates cannot support accurate dashboard-wide year or genre filtering.

Update dates & missing data

The warehouse completion time marks a successful build. The export time marks when its bundle was generated. TMDB dates are extractor-local capture dates, not precise timestamps. IMDb capture time is unavailable.

A warning appears when the warehouse build or either market’s capture is more than eight days old. The pipeline replaces the snapshot; this page does not imply historical changes or trends.

Unknown values read “Unavailable.” Empty rankings can mean no records qualified. Qualified decade counts, genre averages and ranked lists describe their stated populations.

Minimum votes & how rankings work

Decade ratings and top release years use IMDb votes when known, otherwise TMDB votes. These are selected-source thresholds, unlike either-source thresholds elsewhere.

Top years may include qualifying release years outside the displayed decade range. Their rounded mean rating determines rank; ties use release year ascending. People ties use person ID; recommendation ties end with movie ID. These stable tie-breakers do not imply a meaningful difference in quality. Tiny differences between averages should not be treated as evidence of a meaningful quality gap; uncertainty estimates are not available.
